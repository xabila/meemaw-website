Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

Summary: Meemaw respects your privacy. All your dictations and progress stay on your device. We collect, store, and share no personal data whatsoever.

1. Introduction

Meemaw is a dictation learning app built with privacy as an absolute priority. This policy explains how we handle your information.

2. Data collected

We collect no personal data.

Meemaw runs entirely on your device and stores locally:

  • Your dictation progress and scores
  • Your user preferences (voice speed, rules selected)
  • Your custom avatars and user profiles

We do not have servers that receive this data. It never leaves your device.

3. Speech Recognition

Meemaw uses Apple's built-in Speech Recognition framework. Voice data processing is handled securely by your device's operating system in accordance with Apple's privacy policies.

4. Analytics and Tracking

We do not track you. We do not use third-party analytics tools (like Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or Facebook Pixel) inside the app. Your usage remains completely private.

5. In-App Purchases

If you choose to purchase Meemaw PRO (Lifetime, Yearly, or Monthly), the transaction is processed entirely by Apple via the App Store. We use RevenueCat to validate your purchase status, but this relies entirely on an anonymous Apple receipt. We do not know your name, payment details, or email address.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can contact us at xlarrodeapp+meemaw@gmail.com.
