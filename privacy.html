Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Summary: Meemaw, Meemaw Math, and Meemaw Hunter respect your privacy. Learning progress and Screen Time rules stay on your device, all camera/hand gesture processing in Meemaw Hunter runs 100% locally in volatile RAM (zero camera or face data collected, 0s retention), and only anonymous EU-hosted telemetry (Mixpanel EU, ip=0) and anonymous purchase unlocks (StoreKit 2 / RevenueCat) are used—never linked to your identity and with zero tracking. Full 8-language policy for Meemaw Hunter: meemaw.app/hunter/privacy.html.

1. Introduction

Meemaw (Dictation & Spelling), Meemaw Math (Mental Math, Automatismes & Brevet), and Meemaw Hunter (60 FPS Front-Camera Arcade Shooter) are iOS & iPadOS apps built with privacy as a core priority. This policy explains how your information is handled across our apps.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Both Meemaw and Meemaw Math run locally on your iPhone or iPad and store:

  • Your exercise progress, scores, mistake notebook, and preferences locally on your device
  • Leaderboards, achievements, and challenge scores via Apple Game Center (managed by Apple's secure infrastructure under your Apple ID)

3. Screen Time & Parental Control (Apple FamilyControls)

If you choose to enable the optional Screen Time Reward feature ("Contrôle Parental / Temps d'écran"), our apps use Apple's privacy-preserving FamilyControls, ManagedSettings, and DeviceActivity frameworks:

  • App selection tokens are opaque system tokens managed exclusively by iOS on your device.
  • We cannot see which specific apps you use or block, and no Screen Time data ever leaves your device.
  • Parental PIN codes are stored locally in the encrypted iOS Keychain on your device.

4. Speech Recognition

When using voice input, Meemaw uses Apple's built-in Speech Recognition framework. Voice data processing is handled securely by your device's operating system in accordance with Apple's privacy policies.

5. Anonymous Product Analytics (GDPR / EU Hosted)

To improve exercise difficulty and app stability, we collect strictly anonymous, aggregated usage events (such as completed exercise counts and onboarding steps) via Mixpanel EU (data residency in the European Union). We do not collect your name, email, IP address, IDFA, or cross-app advertising identifiers, and we never display third-party ads.

6. In-App Purchases

If you choose to purchase Meemaw PRO or Meemaw Math PRO (Monthly, Yearly, or Lifetime), the transaction is processed entirely by Apple via the App Store. We use RevenueCat solely to validate anonymous Apple purchase receipts and unlock your PRO status across devices. We do not have access to your name, payment card details, or billing address.

7. Meemaw Hunter — 100% On-Device Hand Tracking & Zero Face Data Collected

Meemaw Hunter (Bundle ID com.xlarrodeapp.meemawhunter, App Store ID 6831648316) uses your iPhone or iPad front camera strictly to detect 21 hand joints in real time at 60 FPS using an embedded on-device MediaPipeTasksVision model (HandLandmarker / hand_landmarker.task):

  • Zero Face Data Collected (0s Retention, Zero Sharing): Meemaw Hunter collects zero face data. Although the front-facing camera field of view may incidentally include the player's face while tracking their hand, the app exclusively runs the on-device HandLandmarker model in volatile RAM (~16 ms per frame) to detect 21 hand joints and never detects, extracts, stores, retains, or shares any facial geometry, biometric identifiers, or video frames with any third party (retention period: 0 seconds).
  • Anonymous Telemetry & One-Time Unlock (Data Not Linked · No Tracking): High scores, unlocked worlds, and ergonomics stay local in iOS UserDefaults (CA92.1). Anonymous gameplay events are sent to Mixpanel EU (https://api-eu.mixpanel.com, ip=0 IP discarded at ingestion), and the optional one-time Sheriff Full Game Pack (com.xlarrodeapp.meemawhunter.sheriffpack, Family Sharing supported) is unlocked via Apple StoreKit 2 and RevenueCat using only a random anonymous UUID (NSPrivacyTracking = false, zero IDFA, zero ads). See the dedicated 8-language policy (including Section 3 Face Data Policy) at meemaw.app/hunter/privacy.html.
