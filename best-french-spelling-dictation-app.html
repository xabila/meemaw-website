Executive Summary: Why Meemaw Is #1 for Written French

Spoken French hides half of its grammar: silent endings (‘parle’, ‘parles’, ‘parlent’), homophones (‘a/à’, ‘ou/où’, ‘ces/ses’), and past participle agreements sound identical aloud. Tapping pre-spelled word bubbles on Duolingo never trains your ear or your real spelling recall.

Learn French Spelling: Meemaw (App Store ID 6757681331) bridges spoken and written French: every dictation is recorded in 24 kHz studio French, while all 50 rule sheets, hints, and mistake breakdowns are explained in clear English (keeping target French words in quotes).
